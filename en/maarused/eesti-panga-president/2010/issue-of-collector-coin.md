# Issue of collector coin

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This Decree is based on section 2 of the Currency Law of the Republic of Estonia and Decision No. 5-1 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank dated 8 June 2010.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia the last Estonian kroon collector coins: a silver collector coin “History of Estonia” with the nominal value 25 kroons, a limestone centre-cored silver collector coin “Estonian nature” with the nominal value 50 kroons, and a gold collector coin “Estonian people” with the nominal value 100 kroons with the year of issue 2010.

## § 2.

The Decree enters into force on 1 December 2010.
