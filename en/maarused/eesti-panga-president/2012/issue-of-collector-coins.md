# Issue of collector coins

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is based on subsection 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of Eesti Pank Act and Decision No 2-2 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank dated 27 March 2012.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the London Olympic Games with nominal value 12 euros and the year of issue 2012.

## § 2.

This decree enters into force on 12 July 2012.
