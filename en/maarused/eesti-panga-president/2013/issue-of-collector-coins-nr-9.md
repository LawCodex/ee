# Issue of collector coins

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This Decree is established on the basis of subsection 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Eesti Pank Act and Decision No 3-4 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank dated 7 May 2013.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin "Estonian delegation/ athletes in the XXII Winter Olympic Games in Sochi", with a nominal value of 10 euros and the year of issue of 2014.

## § 2.

This Decree shall enter into force on 9 January 2014.
