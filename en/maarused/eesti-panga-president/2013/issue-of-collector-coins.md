# Issue of collector coins

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This Decree shall be established on the basis of subsection 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Eesti Pank Act and Decision No 3-4 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank dated 7 May 2013.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the music of Raimond Valgre, with a nominal value of 7 euros and the year of issue of 2013.

## § 2.

This Decree shall enter into force on 7 October 2013.
