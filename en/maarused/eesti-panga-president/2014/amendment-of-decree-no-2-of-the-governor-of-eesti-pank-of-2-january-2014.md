# Amendment of Decree No 2 of the Governor of Eesti Pank of 2 January 2014 “Establishment of reports on operational risk of credit institutions and consolidation groups of credit institutions”

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is based on subsection 91 (1) of the Credit Institutions Act.

The first sentence of subsection 4 “Business line identifier” of section 4 “Description of the structure of the report row” of Appendix 1 “Report on operational risk loss events and incidents” of Decree No 2 of the Governor of Eesti Pank of 2 January 2014 “Establishment of reports on operational risk of credit institutions and consolidation groups of credit institutions” (RT I, 07.01.2014, 10) is amended and worded as follows:\

“Business lines are determined on the basis of Article 317(4) of Regulation (EC) No 575/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council and § 11 of the Insurance Activities Act.”
