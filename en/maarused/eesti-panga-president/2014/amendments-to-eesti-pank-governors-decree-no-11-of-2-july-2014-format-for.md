# Amendments to Eesti Pank Governor’s Decree No. 11 of 2 July 2014 “Format for forwarding the supervisory reports established on the basis of the Banking Regulation”

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This Decree is established on the basis of subsection 91 (1) of the Credit Institutions Act.

**§ 1.** Eesti Pank Governor’s Decree No. 11 of 2 July 2014
“Format for forwarding the supervisory reports established on the basis
of the Banking Regulation” (RT I, 03.07.2014, 40) is amended as
follows:

1) section 4
is amended and worded as follows:

“**§ 4. Codes of reports**

Upon submission of reports in XML data exchange
format, the codes of reports set forth in the file “Codes of reports for
the submission of reports in XML format” available on the webpage http://www.fi.ee/schemas\_ xbrl/ shall be used.”;

2) subsection
8 (1) is amended and worded as follows:

“(1) Monetary values are reflected either
in full euros or with the accuracy of one cent.”;

3) section
8 is supplemented by subsections 3 and 4 in the following wording:

“(3) In the absence of data concerning
the respective report upon submission of reports in XML data exchange format,
an empty report shall be submitted.

(4) In the case specified in subsection 3 of
this section, a credit institution shall not be obliged to submit an empty
report, if the data concerning the respective report are absent for the reason
that the credit institution does not hold an authorisation from the Financial
Supervision Authority to use the method required for the calculation of such
data.”;

4) Annex 1
“Codes of reports for the submission of reports in XML format” is
repealed.

**§ 2.** This Decree enters into force on 31 December 2014.
