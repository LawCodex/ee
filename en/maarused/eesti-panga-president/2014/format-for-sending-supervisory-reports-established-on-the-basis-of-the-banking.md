# Format for sending supervisory reports established on the basis of the Banking Regulation

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is based on subsection 91 (1) of the Credit Institutions Act.

## Chapter 1 — General Provisions

### § 1. Scope of decree

This decree establishes the technical requirements that must be followed when submitting to Finantsinspektsioon the supervisory reports established by the Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/451 of 17 December 2020 laying down implementing technical standards for the application of Regulation (EU) No 575/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to supervisory reporting of institutions and repealing Implementing Regulation (EU) No 680/2014 (OJ L 97, 19.3.2021, p. 1–1955) and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/453 of 15 March 2021 laying down implementing technical standards for the application of Regulation (EU) No 575/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council with regard to the specific reporting requirements for market risk (OJ L 89, 16.3.2021, pp. 3–14) (together the *supervisory reporting standard*).\
\[RT I, 28.05.2021, 1 – in force from 28.06.2021\]

### § 2. Application of decree

The requirements of the decree must be followed by all credit institutions and branches of foreign credit institutions operating in Estonia on whom the reporting obligation has been imposed by the supervisory reporting standard.

## Chapter 2 — Data exchange format and manner of presenting reports

### § 3. Data exchange format of reports

(1) The data exchange format used for the submission of the reports is XBRL (*eXtensible Business Reporting Language*).\

### § 5. Sending of reports

Reports are sent to the e-mail address fiaruanded@fi.ee.\
\[RT I, 19.06.2018, 1 - entry into force 01.07.2018\]

### § 6. Use of classifications, codes and international standards

(1) The currency code is indicated in capital letters and in accordance with the ISO 4217 currency code list.

(2) The country code is indicated in capital letters and in accordance with the ISO 3166 two-letter code list for countries and territories.

(3) Entities, holding companies, counterparties or related parties are identified with the commercial registry code, unless otherwise required in the supervisory reporting standard. If there is no commercial registry code, the credit institution defines a suitable identifier.

(4) Identifiers defined by the credit institution, as described in subsection 3, and the text of the reports may not contain the symbols: >, <, &, ', ".

(1) Monetary values are rounded to the nearest euro or cent.

(2) Percentage values are given in the form i/100 rounded to four decimals.
