# Issue of Miina Härma Collector Coin

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This Decree is based on subsection 14<sup>1</sup>(2) of the Bank of Estonia (Eesti Pank) Act and Decision No. 2-2 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 18 March 2014.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the work of Miina Härma with the nominal value of 10 euros and the year of issue 2014.

## § 2.

This Decree shall enter into force on 13 June 2014.
