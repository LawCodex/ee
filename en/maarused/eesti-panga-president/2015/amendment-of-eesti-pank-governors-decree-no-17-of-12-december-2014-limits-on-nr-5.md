# Amendment of Eesti Pank Governor’s Decree No 17 of 12 December 2014 “Limits on granting housing loans and maximum loan maturity”

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This Decree is established on the basis of subsection 83 (2<sup>1</sup>) of the Credit Institutions Act.

**§ 1.** Eesti Pank Governor’s Decree No 17 of 12 December 2014 “Limits on granting housing loans and maximum loan maturity” (RT I, 08.04.2015, 14) is amended as follows:\

 
 1) the following subsection 1<sup>1</sup> is inserted in section 1 of the Decree:\

“(1<sup>1</sup>) The requirements specified in subsection 1 of this section must be met at the moment when the amount of a housing loan (or, in the event of granting the housing loan in instalments, the first instalment of the loan amount) is granted to the disposal of the borrower.”;\

2) subsection 3 (2) of the Decree is amended and worded as follows:\

“(2) The loan-to-value ratio may be up to 85%.”;\

3) subsection 4 (2) of the Decree is amended and worded as follows:\

“(2) The debt service-to-income ratio may be up to 50%.”;\

4) section 6 of the Decree is amended and worded as follows:\

“§ 6. Limit for permitted exceptions\

Exceeding the limits specified in section 3 and/or 4 of this Decree and/or for a longer maturity than set forth in section 5, a credit institution may issue up to 15% of the total amount of new housing loans issued within one quarter.”.\
\

**§ 2.** This Decree enters into force on 1 April 2016.
