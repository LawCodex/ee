# Amendment of Eesti Pank Governor’s Decree No 2 of 2 January 2014 “Establishment of reports on operational risk of credit institutions and consolidation groups of credit institutions”

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This Decree is based on subsection 91 (1) of the Credit Institutions Act.

## § 1.

Appendix 1 “Report on operational risk loss events and incidents” of the Governor of Eesti Pank Decree No 2 of 2 January 2014 “Establishment of reports on operational risk of credit institutions and consolidation groups of credit institutions” (RT I, 24.07.2014, 7) is amended and recast (enclosed).

## § 2. Entry into force of Decree

This Decree enters into force on 1 April 2015.
