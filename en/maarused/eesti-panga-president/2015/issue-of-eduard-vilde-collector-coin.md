# Issue of Eduard Vilde Collector Coin

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This Decree is established on the basis of subsection 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Bank of Estonia (Eesti Pank) Act and Decision No 1-1 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank dated 10 February 2015.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the work of Eduard Vilde with the nominal value of 10 euros and the year of issue 2015.

## § 2.

This Decree enters into force on 3 March 2015.
