# Issue of collector coin dedicated to Jaan Poska

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This Decree is based on subsection 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Eesti Pank Act and Decision No 6-1 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank dated 8 September 2015.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to Jaan Poska with a nominal value of ten euros and the year of issue 2016.

## § 2.

This Decree enters into force on 22 January 2016.
