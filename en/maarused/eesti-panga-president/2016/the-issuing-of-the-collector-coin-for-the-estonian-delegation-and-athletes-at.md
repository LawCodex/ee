# The issuing of the collector coin for the Estonian delegation and athletes at the Games of the XXXI Olympiad in Rio de Janeiro

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of clause 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 7-1 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 13 October 2015.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a collector coin for the Estonian delegation and athletes at the Games of the XXXI Olympiad in Rio de Janeiro with a nominal value of ten euros and the year of issue 2016.

## § 2.

This Decree enters into force on 9 June 2016.
