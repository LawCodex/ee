# The list of other systemically important credit institutions and the requirement for the other systemically important institutions buffer

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of section 86<sup>48</sup> (3) and (7) of the Credit Institutions Act.

## § 1.

Other systemically important institutions operating in Estonia in the meaning of section 86<sup>48</sup> of the Credit Institutions Act are:

1\) Swedbank AS

2\) AS SEB Pank

3\) AS LHV Pank

\[RT I, 05.12.2017, 12 - entry into force 01.01.2018\]

4\) Luminor Bank AS

\[RT I, 22.05.2018, 17 - entry into force 01.07.2018\]

5\) Coop Pank AS

6\) Bigbank AS

\[RT I, 06.12.2024, 1 - entry into force 01.01.2025\]

## § 2.

The credit institutions named in Point 1 must hold systemically important institution buffers at the following rates:

1\) Swedbank AS: 2%

2\) AS SEB Pank: 2%

3\) AS LHV Pank: 2%

\[RT I, 29.11.2022, 5 - entry into force 01.01.2023\]

4\) Luminor Bank AS: 2%

\[RT I, 22.05.2018, 17 - entry into force 01.07.2018\]

5\) Coop Pank AS: 0,5%

6\) Bigbank AS: 0,5%

\[RT I, 06.12.2024, 1 - entry into force 01.01.2025\]

## § 3.

The other systemically important institution buffer requirement is applied to the total risk exposure as calculated under Article 92 Section 3 of Regulation (EU) No 575/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 June 2013 on prudential requirements for credit institutions and investment firms and amending Regulation (EU) No 648/2012 (OJ L 176, 27.06.2013, p 1-337).

## § 4.

The other systemically important institution buffer must be met individually and at a consolidated level. If the credit institution is part of a consolidation group, the requirement that is to be fulfilled on a consolidated basis must be fulfilled at the highest consolidation level in Estonia of that consolidation group.

\[RT I, 29.11.2022, 5 - entry into force 01.01.2023\]
