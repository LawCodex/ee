# Issue of a silver collector coin and a gold collector coin for the 100th anniversary of the Republic of Estonia

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of clause 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 7-1 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 19 June 2017.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the 100th anniversary of the Republic of Estonia with a nominal value of 10 euros and a gold collector coin dedicated to the 100th anniversary of the Republic of Estonia with a nominal value of 100 euros, both with the year of issue 2018.

## § 2.

This Decree enters into force on 21 February 2018.
