# Issuing of the gold and silver collector coins for Hanseatic Tallinn

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of § 14<sup>1</sup> of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 4-3 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 26 March 2016.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to Hanseatic Tallinn with a nominal value of eight euros and a gold collector coin dedicated to Hanseatic Tallinn with a nominal value of 25 euros, both with the year of issue 2017.

## § 2.

This Decree enters into force on 9 February 2017.
