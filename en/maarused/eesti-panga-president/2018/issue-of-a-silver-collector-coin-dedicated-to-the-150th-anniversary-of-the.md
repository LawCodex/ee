# Issue of a silver collector coin dedicated to the 150th anniversary of the birth of Jaan Tõnisson

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of clause 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 2-2 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 13 March 2018.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the 150th anniversary of the birth of Jaan Tõnisson with a nominal value of 15 euros and the year of issue 2018.

## § 2.

This Decree enters into force on 4 December 2018.
