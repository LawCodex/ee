# Issue of a silver collector coin dedicated to the 150th anniversary of the birth of Jüri Jaakson

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of clause 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 4-1 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 16 April 2019.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the 150th anniversary of the birth of Jüri Jaakson with a nominal value of 15 euros and the year of issue 2018.

## § 2.

This decree enters into force on 10 January 2020.
