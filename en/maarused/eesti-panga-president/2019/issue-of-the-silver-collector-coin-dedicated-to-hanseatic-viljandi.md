# Issue of the silver collector coin dedicated to Hanseatic Viljandi

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of clause 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 8-1 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 30 October 2018.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to Hanseatic Viljandi with a nominal value of eight euros and the year of issue 2019.

## § 2.

This decree enters into force on 8 June 2019.
