# Issue of the silver collector coin dedicated to the 200th anniversary of the birth of Johann Voldemar Jannsen

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of clause 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 6-1 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 27 June 2018.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the 200th anniversary of the birth of Johann Voldemar Jannsen with a nominal value of 12 euros and the year of issue 2019.

## § 2.

This Decree enters into force on 16 May 2019.
