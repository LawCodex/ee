# Issue of a collector coin dedicated to the 150th anniversary of the birth of Friedrich Karl Akel

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of clause 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 4-1 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 26 May 2020.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the 150th anniversary of the birth of Friedrich Karl Akel with a nominal value of 15 euros and the year of issue 2021.

## § 2.

This decree enters into force on 2 September 2021.
