# Issue of a silver collector coin for the Estonian athletes at the XXIV Olympic Winter Games in Beijing

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of clause 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 3-5 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 6 April 2021.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the Estonian athletes at the XXIV Olympic Winter Games in Beijing with a nominal value of eight euros and the year of issue 2022.

## § 2.

This decree enters into force on 6 January 2022.
