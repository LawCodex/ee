# Approval of the harmonised conditions for participation in TARGET-Eesti

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of clause 2 (2) 4), clause 14 5), and clause 191 (1) of the Eesti Pank Act.\
\[RT I, 08.03.2023, 7 – entry into force 11.03.2023\]

## § 1. This decree approves the harmonised conditions for participation in TARGET-Eesti as follows:

1\) General terms and conditions for participation in TARGET-Eesti (Annex 1);

1\) General terms and conditions for participation in TARGET-Eesti (Annex 1);

2\) Special terms and conditions for main cash accounts (Annex 2);

3\) Special terms and conditions for real-time gross settlement dedicated cash accounts (Annex 3);

4\) Special terms and conditions for TARGET2-securities dedicated cash accounts (Annex 4);

5\) Special terms and conditions for TARGET instant payment settlement (TIPS) dedicated cash accounts (Annex 5);

6\) Special terms and conditions for ancillary systems (AS) using real-time gross settlement ancillary system (RTGS AS) settlement procedures (Annex 6);

7\) Special terms and conditions for ancillary systems using the TARGET instant payment settlement (TIPS) ancillary system settlement procedure (Annex 7);

## § 2. \[Omitted from this text\].

## § 3. This decree enters into force on 20 March 2023.

\[RT I, 11.11.2022, 5 – entry into force 14.11.2022\]
