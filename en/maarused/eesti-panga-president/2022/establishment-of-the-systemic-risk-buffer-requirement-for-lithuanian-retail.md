# Establishment of the systemic risk buffer requirement for Lithuanian retail exposures secured by residential property

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of subsection 86<sup>49</sup> (2)1) and (4)4) of the Credit Institutions Act.

## § 1.

All credit institutions authorised in Estonia must apply a 2% systemic risk buffer rate for all retail exposures to natural persons resident in Lithuania that are secured by residential property when calculating the systemic risk buffer.

## § 2.

The requirement in paragraph 1 is applied cumulatively with other systemic risk buffer requirements that apply in Estonia.

## § 3.

This Decree enters into force on 1 July 2022.
