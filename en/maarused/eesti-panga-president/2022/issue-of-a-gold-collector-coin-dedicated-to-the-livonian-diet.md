# Issue of a gold collector coin dedicated to the Livonian Diet

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of clause 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 3-4 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 6 April 2021.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a gold collector coin dedicated to the Livonian Diet with a nominal value of 25 euros and the year of issue 2022.

## § 2.

This Decree enters into force on 25 October 2022.
