# The countercyclical capital buffer rate

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of section 86<sup>46</sup> (2) of the Credit Institutions Act.

## § 1.

This decree sets the countercyclical capital buffer rate for the credit risk positions of credit institutions based in Estonia at 1.5%.

## § 2.

Decree No 16 of the Governor of Eesti Pank of 29 November 2021 “The countercyclical capital buffer rate” (RT I, 03.12.2021, 3) is repealed.

## § 3.

This decree enters into force on 1 December 2023.
