# Issue of a collector coin dedicated to the 150th anniversary of the birth of Konstantin Konik

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of clause 14<sup>1</sup>(2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 9-1 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 15 November 2022.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the 150th anniversary of the birth of Konstantin Konik with a nominal value of 15 euros and the year of issue 2023.

## § 2.

This Decree enters into force on 12 December 2023.
