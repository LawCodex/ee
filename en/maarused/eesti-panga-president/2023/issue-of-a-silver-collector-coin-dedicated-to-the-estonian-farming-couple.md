# Issue of a silver collector coin dedicated to the Estonian farming couple

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of clause 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 5-2 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 10 May 2022.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the Estonian farming couple with a nominal value of 14 euros and the year of issue 2023.

## § 2.

This decree enters into force on 6 June 2023.
