# Issue of a collector coin dedicated to the 150th anniversary of the birth of Konstantin Päts

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of clause 14<sup>1</sup>(2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 9-2 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 15 November 2022.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the 150th anniversary of the birth of Konstantin Päts with a nominal value of 15 euros and the year of issue 2024.

## § 2.

This Decree enters into force on 23 February 2024.
