# Issue of a silver collector coin for the Estonian athletes at the Games of the XXXIII Olympiad in Paris

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of clause 14<sup>1</sup>(2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 4-2 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 16 May 2023.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the Estonian athletes at the Games of the XXXIII Olympiad in Paris with a nominal value of ten euros and the year of issue 2024.

## § 2.

This Decree enters into force on 6 May 2024.
