# Issue of the gold and silver collector coins dedicated to Hanseatic Tartu

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of § 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 2-5 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 21 March 2023.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to Hanseatic Tartu with a nominal value of eight euros and a gold collector coin dedicated to Hanseatic Tartu with a nominal value of 25 euros, both with the year of issue 2024.

## § 2.

This decree enters into force on 11 June 2024.
