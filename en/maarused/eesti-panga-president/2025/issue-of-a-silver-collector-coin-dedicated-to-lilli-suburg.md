# Issue of a silver collector coin dedicated to Lilli Suburg

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of clause 14<sup>1</sup>(2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 5-1 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 14 May 2024.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to Lilli Suburg with a nominal value of 15 euros and the year of issue 2025.

## § 2.

This decree enters into force on 21 November 2025.
