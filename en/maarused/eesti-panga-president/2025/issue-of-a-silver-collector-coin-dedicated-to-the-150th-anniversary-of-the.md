# Issue of a silver collector coin dedicated to the 150th anniversary of the birth of Otto Strandman

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of § 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 6-2 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 11 June 2024.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the 150th anniversary of the birth of Otto Strandman with a nominal value of 15 euros and the year of issue 2025.

## § 2.

This Decree enters into force on 4 December 2025.
