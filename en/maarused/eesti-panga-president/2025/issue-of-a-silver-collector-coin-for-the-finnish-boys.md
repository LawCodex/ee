# Issue of a silver collector coin for the Finnish Boys

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of clause 14<sup>1</sup>(2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 6-1 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 11 June 2024.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the Finnish Boys with a nominal value of 15 euros and the year of issue 2025.

## § 2.

This Decree enters into force on 30 October 2025.
