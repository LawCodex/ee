# Reporting requirements concerning holdings of securities of banking groups

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This Decree is established on the basis of § 2 (2) 7) of the Eesti Pank Act, and § 91 (3) of the Credit Institutions Act.

## Chapter 1 — General Provisions

### § 1. Scope of regulation

This Decree establishes:

1\) the requirements for the content and structure of the reports referred to in § 3(1) of this Decree (hereinafter the reports) to be observed in reporting data on holdings of securities as defined in Article 3a of Regulation (EU) No 1011/2012 of the European Central Bank concerning statistics on holdings of securities (OJ L 305, 1.11.2012, pp 6–24);

2\) the due dates and procedures for submitting to Eesti Pank the reports specified in § 3 (1) of this Decree.

### § 2. Scope of application

The reports provided for by this Decree shall be compiled and submitted by banking groups operating in Estonia, as defined in Article 2(1)b) of Regulation (EU) No 1011/2012 of the European Central Bank.

## Chapter 2 — Reports, reporting principles and submission of reports

### § 3. Reports and reporting principles

(1) Banking groups shall compile the following reports and submit them to Eesti Pank:

1\) ‘Report on holdings of non-ISIN securities’ with code 2230 (Appendix 1);

2\) ‘Group-level report on holdings of securities’ with code 2231 (Appendix 2);

3\) ‘Entity-level report on holdings of securities’ with code 2232 (Appendix 3);

(2) The data shall be compiled in conformity with the requirements set out in Article 3a of Regulation (EU) No 1011/2012 of the European Central Bank.

(3) In Appendixes 1–3 of this Decree the terms shall be used as defined in Annex II of Regulation (EU) No 1011/2012 of the European Central Bank.

(4) For the purposes of the report described in Annex 1 of the Decree, the issuer shall be assigned to an institutional sector following Chapter 2 of Annex A to Regulation (EU) No 549/2013 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 21 May 2013 on the European system of national and regional accounts in the European Union (OJ L 174, 26.06.2013, pp 1–727).

### § 4. Reference period and due dates for submission of reports

(1) The reference period shall be a quarter.

(2) The reports are submitted by the twentieth banking day after the end of the reference period.

### § 5. Submission of reports

(1) The reports shall be submitted to Eesti Pank electronically in the XML (eXtensible Markup Language) format in accordance with Eesti Pank Governor's Decree No 4 of 29 May 2018 “Requirements for the electronic submission of reports” (RT I, 05.06.2018, 1).

(2) If errors are detected or the accounting principles are changed, new reports shall be submitted electronically with the required amendments.

(3) The text and data attributes of the reports may not contain the symbols: >, <, &, ', ".

## Chapter 3 — Implementing Provision

### § 6. Repeal of the Decree

Decree No 7 of the Governor of Eesti Pank of 20 June 2017 “Reporting requirements concerning holdings of securities of banking groups” (RT I, 28.05.2021, 3) is repealed.

### § 7. Entry into force of the Decree

This decree enters into force on 1 January 2026.
