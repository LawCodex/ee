# The list of statistical actions of Eesti Pank under the official statistical programme

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

The decree is passed under clause 16 (1) 2) of the Official Statistics Act.

## § 1. Approval of the list of statistical actions of Eesti Pank

The decree approves the list of statistical actions of Eesti Pank under the official statistical programme 2025–2029 (annexed).

## § 2. Repeal of the Decree

This decree repeals Decree No 1 of the Governor of Eesti Pank of 9 January 2024 “List of statistical actions of Eesti Pank under the official statistical programme” (RT I, 12.01.2024, 2).

Madis Müller, Governor
