# Establishing reporting for the Household Finance and Consumption Survey

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established under § 2(2)7) of the Eesti Pank Act, § 91(3) of the Credit Institutions Act, § 30<sup>1</sup>(3<sup>1</sup>) of the Official Statistics Act, Article 5 of the Statute of the European System of Central Banks and of the European Central Bank, and Article 4 of Council Regulation (EC) No 2533/98 of 23 November 1998 concerning the collection of statistical information by the European Central Bank.

## Chapter 1 — General Provisions

### § 1. Scope of the Decree

(1) This decree establishes requirements for the contents and form of the reports listed in § 3 of this decree that are needed for Eesti Pank to carry out its tasks, and the due dates and procedure for submitting those reports to Eesti Pank.

(2) The reports required under the decree are used to meet the requirements set in Guideline (EU) 2025/333 of the European Central Bank of 31 January 2025 on statistical information to be reported on household wealth, income and consumption (ECB/2025/3) (OJ L 2025/333, 27.2.2025).

### § 2. Application of the Decree

(1) All credit institutions and branches of credit institutions operating in Estonia (‘Credit Institutions’) are obliged to prepare and submit the reports provided in this decree.

(2) Eesti Pank may waive the requirement for a Credit Institution to submit one or several reports listed in this decree if the Credit Institution has not submitted any data on the loan liabilities, deposits or securities of resident private individuals by the end of the reporting year under Decree No 16 of the Governor of Eesti Pank of 9 November 2007 “Establishment of off-balance-sheet reporting of credit institutions” or Decree No 3 of the Governor of Eesti Pank of 13 May 2025 “Establishment of supplementary reports on a credit institution’s balance sheet”.

(3) Information on the reporting requirement set under this decree can be found on the data submission portal of Eesti Pank and Finantsinspektsioon on the first working day of the next month.

## Chapter 2 — Reports, principles for preparing reports, and submission of reports

### § 3. Reports and reporting principles

(1) Credit institutions shall compile and submit to Eesti Pank the following reports:

1\) loan liabilities of individuals in the sample (Annex 1);

2\) deposits of individuals in the sample (Annex 2);

3\) securities of individuals in the sample (Annex 3).

(2) Terms are used in the Appendixes to this decree as defined in Decree No 3 of the Governor of Eesti Pank of 13 May 2025 “Establishment of supplementary reports on a credit institution’s balance sheet”.

(3) The reports are compiled in euros and rounded off to integers.

(4) Reports must show interest rates in the format of interest rate divided by one hundred (i/100) and rounded to four decimal places.

(5) Dates in the reports are indicated using the date format yyyy/mm/dd.

(6) Amounts recorded in foreign currency must be converted into euros at the official exchange rate of the European Central Bank for the last banking day of the reporting period.

### § 4. Defining the authorised person and submitting the survey codes

(1) The Credit Institution sends the name, personal identification number and email address of the person authorised to receive pseudonymous survey codes to the email address aruandlus@eestipank.ee of Eesti Pank no later than 10 September of the calendar year following the reporting period.

(2) Eesti Pank sends the personal identification codes of the people in the survey sample and the corresponding pseudonymous survey codes in encrypted form to the authorised person of the Credit Institution no later than 30 September of the calendar year following the reporting period.

(3) The Credit Institution informs Eesti Pank immediately if the authorised person changes.

### § 5. Reporting period and frequency and deadlines for submissions

(1) The reporting period is the calendar year. The data are submitted as at the end of the period.

(2) Data are compiled for the first time for the reports as at 31 December 2026 and for the second time as at 31 December 2028. The reports are then compiled every three years as at the end of the reporting period.

(3) The reports are submitted no later than 30 November of the calendar year following the reporting period.

### § 6. Submission of reports

(1) The reports shall be submitted to Eesti Pank electronically in the XML (eXtensible Markup Language) format, as in Decree No. 4 of the Governor of Eesti Pank of 29 May 2018 “Requirements for the electronic submission of reports”.

(2) The reports submitted may only use the pseudonymous survey codes. Personal identification codes may not be reported.

(3) If errors have been found or accounting principles have been changed, corrected reports shall be submitted electronically.

(4) If there are no data available for a particular report, a blank report is submitted.

## Chapter 3 — Implementing Provisions

### § 7. Entry into force and application of the Decree

This Decree enters into force on 1 July 2026.
