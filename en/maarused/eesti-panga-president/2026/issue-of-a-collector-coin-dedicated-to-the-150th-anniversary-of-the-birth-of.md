# Issue of a collector coin dedicated to the 150th anniversary of the birth of Georg Lurich

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of § 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 6-1 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 10 June 2025.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the 150th anniversary of the birth of Georg Lurich with a nominal value of 15 euros and the year of issue 2026.

## § 2.

This Decree enters into force on 22 April 2026.
