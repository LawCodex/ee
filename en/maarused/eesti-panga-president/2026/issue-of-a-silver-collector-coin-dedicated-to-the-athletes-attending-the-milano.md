# Issue of a silver collector coin dedicated to the athletes attending the Milano Cortina 2026 Olympic Winter Games

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This decree is established on the basis of § 14<sup>1</sup> (2) of the Eesti Pank Act and decision No 3-6 of the Supervisory Board of Eesti Pank of 25 March 2025.

## § 1.

To issue in the Republic of Estonia a silver collector coin dedicated to the athletes attending the Milano Cortina 2026 Olympic Winter Games with a nominal value of 15 euros and the year of issue 2026.

## § 2.

This decree enters into force on 23 January 2026.
