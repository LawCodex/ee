# Labelling Requirements for Crystal Glass Products

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

*1*

The regulation is established on the basis of subsection 7<sup>1</sup> of § 5 of the Consumer Protection Act.

## § 1. Scope of application

(1) The regulation applies to the crystal glass products listed under heading No 7013 of the goods nomenclature established by Council Regulation (EEC) No 2658/87 on the tariff and statistical nomenclature and on the Common Customs Tariff (OJ L 256, 7.9.1987, p. 1–675) (hereinafter the product).

(2) The regulation does not apply to exported products.

## § 2. Product labelling

(1) A manufacturer or distributor may use the description and symbol of the category provided in Annex 1 on a product marketed in their economic or professional activities, provided that the product corresponds in its characteristics to the description of the relevant product category provided in Annex 1.

(2) Where the trade mark of a product, the business name of the manufacturer or distributor or other product labelling includes the symbol provided in Annex 1 or a designation confusingly similar to that symbol, the manufacturer or distributor is obliged to clearly provide information:

1\) in the explanation or description of the characteristics of the product accompanying the product, about the conformity of the product with the characteristics described in Annex 1;

2\) in the explanatory report, about the exact characteristics of the product if the product does not correspond to the characteristics described in Annex 1.

(3) The description of the characteristics of the product and the symbol set out in Annex 1 may be on a single label of the product or in other explanatory material accompanying the product.

## § 3. Methods for determining product characteristics

The conformity of a product bearing the description and symbol of the category of product specified in subsection 1 of § 2 with the characteristics described in Annex 1 is demonstrated by the use of methods defined in Annex 2.
