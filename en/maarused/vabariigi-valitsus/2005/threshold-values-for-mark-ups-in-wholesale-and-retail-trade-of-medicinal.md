# Threshold values for mark-ups in wholesale and retail trade of medicinal products and the procedure for their implementation

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

This Regulation is established on the basis of subsection 15 (1) of the Medicinal Products Act.

## Chapter 1 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

### § 1. Scope of application

This Regulation establishes the threshold values for mark-ups in wholesale and retail trade of medicinal products and the procedure for their implementation.

## Chapter 2 — PROCEDURE FOR IMPLEMENTATION OF THRESHOLD VALUES FOR MARK-UPS

### § 2. Mark-ups in wholesale trade of medicinal products

(1) Proportionate mark-ups shall be applied in wholesale trade of medicinal products. The threshold values of mark-ups are provided in the Annex.

(2) The mark-up in wholesale trade of medicinal products shall not exceed EUR 6.39 per sales packaging.\
\[RT I 2010, 60, 407 - entry into force 01.01.2011\]

### § 3. Mark-ups in retail trade of medicinal products

(1) Proportionate and fixed mark-ups shall be applied in retail trade of medicinal products. The threshold values of mark-ups are provided in the Annex.

(2) The mark-up in retail trade of medicinal products shall not exceed EUR 6.39 per sales packaging.\
\[RT I 2010, 60, 407 - entry into force 01.01.2011\]

(3) The mark-up of medicinal products prepared as magistral formulae and medicinal products prepared as officinal formulae shall consist of proportionate mark-up and fixed mark-up. The proportionate mark-up shall not exceed 50 % of the total cost of the ingredients and packaging of the medicinal product. The fixed mark-up shall not exceed EUR 0.96 per sales packaging.\
\[RT I 2010, 60, 407 - entry into force 01.01.2011\]

### § 4. Retail trade of medicinal products to family physicians, social welfare institutions and hospitals

For retail trade of medicinal products to social welfare institutions and hospitals, the retail price of medicinal products with a retail price lower than EUR 3.20 shall be multiplied with a coefficient of 0.85 and the retail price of medicinal products with a retail price between EUR 3.20 and EUR 31.96 with a coefficient of 0.9. In retail trade of medicinal products to family physicians, these coefficients shall only apply to retail trade of vaccines.\
\[RT I 2010, 60, 407 - entry into force 01.01.2011\]

### § 5. Sale of medicinal products by wholesaler to wholesaler or by pharmacy to pharmacy

When medicinal products are sold by a wholesaler to a wholesaler or by a pharmacy to a pharmacy, the total mark-up applied by the buyer and seller shall not exceed the threshold values for mark-ups provided in the Annex and, in case of medicinal products prepared as magistral formulae, the threshold values for mark-ups provided in subsection 3 (3).

## Chapter 3 — IMPLEMENTING PROVISION

### § 6. Entry into force of the Regulation

The Regulation shall enter into force on 1 March 2005.
