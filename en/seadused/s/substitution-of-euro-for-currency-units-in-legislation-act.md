# Substitution of Euro for Currency Units in Legislation Act

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

## § 1.

Currency units of Member States of the European Union that have adopted euro shall be deemed to be a currency unit euro (EUR) in legislation, except for the acts on foreign loan agreements and ratification of granting state guarantee to loans, taking account of the conversion rates determined by the Council of the European Union.

## § 2.

This Act enters into force on 1 January 2002.
