# The Constitution of the Republic of Estonia Amendment Act

> Unofficial English translation published by Riigi Teataja. Only the Estonian text is legally binding.

The people of Estonia, on the basis of § 162 of the Constitution, by a referendum held on 14 September 2003, adopted the following Act to amend the Constitution of the Republic of Estonia:

## § 1.

Estonia may belong to the European Union, proceeding from the fundamental principles of the Constitution of the Republic of Estonia.

## § 2.

Upon Estonia’s membership in the European Union, the Constitution of the Republic of Estonia shall be applied taking into account the rights and obligations arising from the treaty of accession.

## § 3.

This Act can be amended only by a referendum.

## § 4.

This Act shall enter into force three months after the date of its promulgation.
